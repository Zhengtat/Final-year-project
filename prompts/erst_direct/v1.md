one of the discourse relations below. If both concepts are in one unit, or the passage does not signal a discourse relation between
the units holding them, answer NO_ERST_RELATION.

{relation_options}

earlier; "either order" = the satellite may come before or after; "multinuclear" = two or more equally prominent nuclei, no satellite.

{signal_options}
