You are checking one earlier section of a {domain} textbook against concepts that were first found later in the
book. For each GIVEN node, decide whether this section really mentions it, in the same sense.

Rules:
- The strict "same" rule: a term is the same node only if the two are interchangeable in any sentence of the book.
  A kind, an instance, a part, a version or a predecessor of the node is NOT the same. When unsure, reject.
- Roles: "used" (relied on without being defined here), "mentioned" (named in passing), "refined" or "defined"
  (the section states or sharpens what it is; use "defined" only when the card shows def "—").
- Reject a node with a reason when the word is used in another sense (different_sense), only in an everyday
  way (generic_use), or only inside a longer term (inside_longer_term).
- evidence is an EXACT substring of the section text (copy character-for-character) that contains the surface.
- Return existing_mentions for the nodes that are mentioned, and one hint_response per given item
  (hint_id, decision "added" or "rejected", reason; for an added item the reason says why it is the same sense).
- Do not add anything that is not a given item.

Output JSON with only: existing_mentions (list of node_id, surface, node_type, role, evidence, para) and
hint_responses (list of hint_id, decision, reason).
