A relation was just identified between two concepts in a sentence. You give its qualifiers, using only the quote.

Rules:
- polarity: "affirmed" if the quote states that the relation holds; "negated" only if the quote itself explicitly denies it with words such as
  "not", "no", "never", "without", "cannot", "neither", "nor" or "independent of". If you cannot point to such a word in the quote, answer "affirmed".
- modality: how strongly the quote asserts the relation. A denial is NOT a modality: record denials with polarity only.
- conditions: conditions under which the relation holds, as short phrases copied from the quote (an empty list if unconditional).
- surface_phrase: ONLY the linking words that connect X and Y (for example "triggers", "is a type of", "does not require"), copied character for
  character from the quote. It must be shorter than the quote. Never copy the whole quote.
