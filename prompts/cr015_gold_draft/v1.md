You are annotating a {domain} textbook section for an exhaustive concept benchmark. For every sentence listed below, mark every mention of a
concept, as the guide below defines it. The sentences come from one section, in order; earlier sentences give context.

Guide:
- A concept is a word or short noun phrase naming a knowledge element of the field with a specific meaning in it (a protocol, mechanism, component,
  data unit, parameter, property, event, state, layer, identifier, or a general technical idea). Annotate every concept the sentence names or uses,
  not only important ones.
- Annotate the whole noun phrase as the text uses it, dropping purely general adjectives ("a", "the", "many", "new" unless part of the term).
  Keep technical modifiers ("TCP congestion control", not "the TCP congestion control").
- Split coordinations ("routers and switches" gives two mentions). Abbreviations are separate mentions of the same concept ("Internet Protocol (IP)":
  one mention "Internet Protocol", one mention "IP", same concept).
- Nested concepts: if both an inner and an outer term are valid concepts in that sentence ("congestion control" inside "TCP congestion control"), give
  both mentions and set nested_valid=true on the INNER one. If the inner words are only part of a longer term and not a concept on their own there,
  annotate only the outer term.
- Single words are concepts when the field gives them a specific meaning ("router", "bandwidth", "frame"). Generic discourse words ("approach", "case",
  "way", "example", "issue"), numbers and values, clauses, and lists are not concepts.
- span_text must be copied EXACTLY, character for character, from the sentence. occurrence is 1 for the first occurrence of that text in the sentence,
  2 for the second, and so on.
- concept_name is one canonical name per concept (use the same name for every mention of the same concept, including plurals, abbreviations and short
  forms). Different senses, kinds, parts or versions are different concepts.
- node_type is one of: Protocol, Mechanism, Component, DataUnit, Parameter, Property, Event, State, Layer, Identifier, Concept.
- A sentence with no concept has an empty mentions list. Return every sentence_id given, in order.
