You are preparing the instructions a concept extractor will follow in a new domain. The extractor reads documents of this domain
section by section and lists the domain concepts each section names, to build a knowledge graph. It has no list of concept types:
it relies on a short description of the domain and on the guidance you draft here.

Read the owner's purpose and the sample of documents below, then draft:

1. document_kind: one word for what the documents are (for example paper, textbook, manual, report).
2. domain: a short name of the field (a few words).
3. domain_description: one or two sentences saying what the documents are about and what kind of knowledge they carry, written for
   the extractor.
4. specificity: "named_instances" when, for this purpose, only specific named things count as concepts (a named model, a named
   dataset, a named technique) and general words for kinds of things do not; "field_terms" when general technical terms of the field
   count too (for example the name of a class of devices or processes). Decide from the purpose and from how the documents use their
   terms. specificity_reason: one sentence.
5. categories: for EACH of these kinds of concept, decide whether the documents use it as knowledge the graph should hold (include
   true / false), whether only named ones count (scope "named_only") or general ones too ("any"), up to 4 example terms from the
   sample, and a one-sentence reason:
{category_list}
6. always_concepts: up to 20 terms that are common English words but have a specific technical meaning in this domain and should
   always be extracted when the text uses them in that sense (for example an evaluation measure named by a common word).
7. not_alone: up to 20 generic words of this field that the documents use for a whole area or a kind of thing and that the extractor
   should not extract on their own, only as part of a longer, specific name.
8. acronym_convention: "long_form_with_acronym_alias" (the name is the long form; the acronym is an alias) unless the purpose asks for
   the parenthesised acronym to stay in the span ("keep_parenthesised_acronym_in_span").
9. keep_variant_modifier: true when a modifier naming the variant the text uses belongs in the span ("standard fine-tuning",
   "binary classification"), false when the bare head is the concept.

Rules:
- Every example term, always_concepts term and not_alone term comes from the sample: give the term and an EXACT quote from the sample
  (copy character-for-character, at most 25 words) that contains the term.
- Do not invent terms that are not in the sample. Do not use knowledge of any annotated dataset or benchmark.
- Prefer fewer, clear entries over many doubtful ones.
