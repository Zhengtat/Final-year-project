You are picking the exact relation a sentence states between two concepts, within the family already identified for this pair.

Rules:
- Pick the reading the sentence actually states. If the sentence states OR DENIES a relation, choose that relation: a denial is recorded by the
  polarity qualifier in a later step, so it is not a reason to choose no_relation or other.
- If X and Y are only listed together or co-mentioned, choose no_relation.
- If the sentence says X and Y are two names for the very same thing (a synonym, an alias, "also called"), and the options include same_concept,
  choose same_concept. Do not choose it when the sentence says they differ, or when they are only similar or related.
- Each option shows its reading filled in with the two concepts. direction is "forward" for a reading that names X first, "reversed" for a reading
  that names Y first; send "forward" for symmetric relations, no_relation and other.
- Choose other only if the sentence relates X and Y but no option fits. Then other_description (one sentence saying what the sentence expresses)
  and other_suggested_label (a 1 to 3 word verb phrase, such as "connects" or "is carried in") are REQUIRED. For every other answer both are null.
- For contrasts_with, dimension_quote is REQUIRED: the word or words of the sentence that name what X and Y differ in (for example "faster", or
  "8 bits and 16 bits"). It must be ONE contiguous piece of the sentence, copied character for character. If the sentence names no such words,
  choose no_relation instead. For every other relation dimension_quote is null.

- Roles come first. Each option's definition says what its source (the concept named first in the reading) and its target must be.
  Before choosing, rule out every option whose source and target roles the two concepts cannot play in the chosen direction, judging
  each concept by what it is in this sentence. Choose only among the options that remain. When two remaining options differ only in
  what the source or target is, pick the one whose roles match these concepts; the "not ..." lines under an option show such contrasts.
  If the sentence states a relation but no remaining option fits it, choose other.

- endpoint_fit: after choosing a relation from the options, check the roles its definition gives the two sides. Answer "fits" when the
  concept you put first in the chosen reading can play the source role and the other concept the target role (for example, for "X is
  measured by Y", Y must be something that measures). Answer "does_not_fit" when either concept cannot play its role, even if the sentence
  links them. endpoint_fit_reason: one short sentence naming the roles. For no_relation, same_concept and other, answer "fits" with the
  reason "no relation chosen".

evidence_quote: an EXACT substring of the sentence (copy character-for-character) that states the relation, and it must contain both X and Y (or
their names).
statement: a one-sentence paraphrase of the relation in your own words, naming both concepts.
