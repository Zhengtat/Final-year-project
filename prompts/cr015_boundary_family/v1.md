You are choosing term boundaries in a {domain} textbook. Each FAMILY is one stretch of a sentence (marked with << >>) together with overlapping
CANDIDATE spans taken from inside it. Decide, for EVERY candidate, whether its exact boundaries are a valid term boundary in this sentence.
Do not decide how important a term is. A wrong boundary is not the same as "not a concept": a shorter or longer span may still be right.

Give each candidate exactly one label:
- KEEP_PRIMARY: this exact span is the main term of the stretch, written as the text uses it.
- KEEP_NESTED: this exact span is ALSO a valid independent term inside (or beside) a primary span, so both should be kept
  ("congestion control" inside "TCP congestion control"). It is NOT valid when the extra word merely modifies or wraps the term
  ("effective congestion control" does not make "effective" part of the term, so only the shorter span is a nested keep when the longer one is rejected).
- EXPAND_TO: the right term is LONGER than this candidate; give target_surface, an exact contiguous piece of the sentence (at most 6 words).
- SHRINK_TO: the right term is SHORTER than this candidate; give target_surface, an exact contiguous piece of the sentence.
- REJECT_DESCRIPTIVE: the span is a descriptive phrase, a wrapper around a term, or a sentence fragment ("operation of the routing protocol").
- REJECT_GENERIC: the span is a single everyday or discourse word that cannot be a term boundary by itself here. Do NOT use this for a word that
  could be a technical term in the field; leave that to the later check.
- REJECT_UNSUPPORTED: the span is not a coherent phrase of the sentence.

Rules:
- Return one decision for EVERY candidate_id of every family, in the order given. A family may keep zero, one or several spans.
- target_surface is null unless the label is EXPAND_TO or SHRINK_TO; then it must be copied exactly from the sentence.
- reason_code is one of: correct_boundary, nested_independent, longer_term_in_text, shorter_term, wrapper_phrase, discourse_word, fragment.
- Do not keep a span just because it is a substring of a kept span, and do not keep only the longest span by default.
