You are extracting a graph of claims from a student's short-answer response, to be
reviewed and corrected by a human before it becomes gold-standard data. Extract
what the student actually wrote — do not judge whether it is correct.

Question:
{question}

Known concepts for this question (link to these by exact canonical_name when the
student is clearly referring to one; otherwise leave source/target concept name
null and record the student's own words in the *_surface field):
{known_concepts}

Student's answer:
{answer_text}

Relation guideline (definitions, templates, examples, and what NOT to confuse each
relation with — choose the closest match; use "other" only if truly nothing fits):
{relation_guideline}

Task:

1. **Edges.** List every claim (edge) the student makes as a (source, relation,
   target) triple. For each: source_concept_name/target_concept_name (or null +
   the surface words the student used), relation, polarity, modality, conditions,
   stance (asserted/hedged/questioned/quoted), an evidence_quote (EXACT substring
   of the answer above), extraction_confidence, link_confidence (0 if both concept
   names are null), and:
   - **part_type**: only when relation is "part_of" (component/member/phase),
     else null.
   - **dimension**: only when relation is "contrasts_with", else null.
   - **surface_phrase**: the student's own linking words for this claim — always
     required.

2. **Chain links.** Where the student connects two of their own claims with a
   reason (because / in order to / if...then / then / whereas), propose a chain
   link: from_edge_index and to_edge_index are 0-based positions in the `edges`
   list above (from = the supported claim, to = the supporting claim), a type
   (cause/purpose/condition/sequence/contrast), a one-sentence statement, the
   surface_phrase connective as written (or null), and an evidence_quote (EXACT
   substring covering the reasoning). Only propose a link the student's wording
   actually supports.

Every evidence_quote must be copied character-for-character from the student's
answer above.
