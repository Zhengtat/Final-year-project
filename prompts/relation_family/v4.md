You are checking whether a passage states a relation between two concepts, and if so, which broad
family it belongs to. The passage is one or more sentences from the same text in which both
concepts occur, one sentence per line.

Concept X: {concept_x}
Concept Y: {concept_y}

Passage:
{sentence}

Families (what each covers, and the relations inside it):
{family_options}

Rules:
- If any sentence of the passage states OR DENIES a relation between X and Y, choose the family of that relation.
  A denial (for example "does not need", "is independent of") is recorded later as a qualifier, so
  do not answer no_relation or other for a denial.
- If X and Y are only listed together, only mentioned in the same sentences, or one merely sets the
  scene for the other, choose no_relation.
- Choose other only if the passage really relates X and Y but none of the families above fits.

Give the family and a one-sentence reason.
